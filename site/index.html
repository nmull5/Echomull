EchoMull

Because accessibility shouldn't have a price tag.

See what people around you are saying, as it happens. Each person gets their own color. Made for a family member who is hard of hearing and wants to follow the talk at the kitchen table.

  • Free, nothing locked
  • No ads
  • Stays on your iPhone

What it looks like

What it does

  • Live captions as chat bubblesWords show up as people talk. Make the text as big as you like, bold or high-contrast, in light or dark.
  • A color for each personUp to 4 people at once get their own color and symbol. Tap a bubble to fix a name or color.
  • Saved voicesSave your voice and your family's. EchoMull then shows them by name. It takes three short lines, read in a normal, louder and softer voice.
  • Near ↔ Far sliderSlide toward Near for just the people close to you, or toward Far for the whole room.
  • Type to replyType a reply or pick a quick phrase. It shows in huge letters, and you can flip it for the person across the table.
  • Say it out loudYour phone can speak your typed reply for you. Pick the voice and the speed, or use your own Personal Voice.
  • Table modeTap Table and the screen splits in two. The half facing the other person turns around and shows your reply in big letters.
  • Sound alertsThe screen flashes and your phone vibrates for a doorbell, a knock, a smoke alarm, a siren, a crying baby, a barking dog or someone saying your name. Turn it on in Settings.
  • See how things are saidLittle tags like (laughing) or (shouting), and louder words in bold.
  • Live translationRead captions in another language, with the original underneath. Made on your iPhone.
  • History you controlConversations stay on your phone. Search them, share them as text or a PDF, or delete them. You can turn saving off.
  • A quick tourThe first time you open it, a short tour points at each button and says what it does.
  • Free, for realNothing to buy, no subscription, no time limit, no ads.

Good to know

Not for emergencies. Captions can be wrong. Don't rely on them for medical, legal or safety matters.

  • Captions can be wrong with noise, accents or people talking at once.
  • Colors are a guess. They can change for a second, and a new person may take a few sentences to get their own. Tap a bubble to fix a mistake.
  • It can't caption phone calls or FaceTime. Apple Live Captions can (see below).
  • Near ↔ Far goes by loudness. A loud voice far away can still show up.
  • Sound alerts don't replace smoke alarms or other safety devices. They work only while EchoMull is listening. When it's closed, your iPhone's own Sound Recognition can alert you.
  • Captions stop when you close the app, and when the phone locks unless Keep listening in the background is on.
  • Translation needs iOS 18 or later. Each language is a free, one-time download.
  • The first setup needs internet for a one-time download of about 100 MB or more. Wi-Fi is best. After that it works without internet.
  • Needs iOS 17 or later. Works best on iPhone 12 or newer with iOS 26.

Privacy in short

  • Everything stays on your iPhone: audio, captions, typed replies and saved voices. We never get any of it, and we have no servers.
  • Saved voices are recorded only when you choose. Ask before you save someone else's voice. You can delete any voice at any time.
  • The app goes online only to download files it needs: Apple's speech and translation files, and a one-time download for speaker colors from Hugging Face. Nothing about you is sent.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account and no analytics. The app collects no data. This website has no cookies, scripts or trackers.

Other free options

These cost nothing, and several are really good. EchoMull's extras are the speaker colors, saved voices and the Near ↔ Far slider. Use whatever works best for you.

Apple Live Captions (built into the iPhone)

On iPhone 11 or later, open Settings > Accessibility > Live Captions. It captions the room, phone calls and FaceTime. In the room it doesn't show who is talking, and it works in fewer languages.

Rylo Live Transcribe

Free, no ads, no time limits, works offline, 50+ languages. It's part of the "Rylo: Live Call Captioning" app. It doesn't tell speakers apart in person.

NALscribe

A free app from Australia's National Acoustic Laboratories, with 13 languages. Works offline on some phones.

Phone calls in the US

Captioned phone (IP CTS), IP Relay and video relay (VRS) are free if you have hearing loss. Find providers on the FCC's list at fcc.gov.
